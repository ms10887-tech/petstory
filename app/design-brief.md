# The Pet Story Co. — Design Brief

## Design read
A warm, cinematic pet-story brand for dog owners who learn better through story than lectures. The emotional register is gentle, sincere, and trustworthy — not salesy, loud, or cartoonish.

## Concept spine
"Every pet problem has a story behind it." The site reads like a storybook opened on a table: warm paper tones, cinematic depth, soft edges, and a sense of unfolding narrative. Each page is a chapter. Each recommendation is earned through the story, not sold.

## Delivery tier
cinema — scroll-driven chapters with a cinematic hero, soft reveals, and a story-like rhythm.

## Locked palette
- **Background:** warm paper white (#FAF8F4) as primary; soft cream (#F3EFE7) for section alternation
- **Text:** deep charcoal (#1C1B1A) for primary; warm gray (#5B5854) for secondary
- **Accent:** muted terracotta (#B8654A) — warm, earthy, not neon, not aggressive
- **Secondary accent:** sage green (#7A8C6F) — a second earth tone for badges and accent elements
- **Border/line:** warm stone (#D8D3C9)

Defense: warm paper-white base feels like a book or a cherished photo album. Terracotta as the single accent is warm, organic, and emotionally grounded — perfect for a pet story brand. Sage green adds a natural second tone without fighting. No neon, no purple, no pure black. This palette lives in the world of a living room at golden hour.

## Locked type
- **Display:** Outfit (clean warm geometric sans, friendly but premium)
- **Body:** Outfit (same family, well-behaved at reading sizes)
- **Mono:** IBM Plex Mono (for small UI bits, numbers, meta labels)

Justification: Outfit is modern and warm without being childish or cartoonish. It reads as premium but approachable — exactly the tone of cinematic storytelling for pet owners. Serif is deliberately avoided — this is a film brand, not a book publisher.

## Tier-1 technique
A2 — Layered depth with scroll-driven parallax. The hero features a layered illustration (Dad Dog + Puppy in a room, built as stacked layers) that drifts at different speeds as you scroll. It communicates depth and story, like a diorama coming to life.

Defense: matches the "storybook" concept spine — layers of a scene unfurling as you read. Not over-the-top spectacle, but enough visual richness to feel premium and cinematic.

## Section plan
Home page sections (8 total):
1. Hero — split: left headline + story hook, right hero visual (50/50 split)
2. Philosophy / about — left-aligned narrative text, medium measure
3. Meet the characters — asymmetric 2-card grid (Dad Dog + Puppy)
4. How it works — 3-step story flow, horizontal with connecting line
5. Featured story / latest video — large featured card with play overlay
6. Recommended reads — 3 blog post cards in a row (bento-style heights)
7. Email signup — split: value prop left, form right
8. Footer — 3 columns: brand, explore, legal

## Asset plan
Hero visual: cinematic 3D-style illustration of Dad Dog and Puppy in a warm living room, golden hour light. Two versions — one for the hero frame, one as a layered parallax asset.

Section plates:
- Characters: two individual portrait-style illustrations (Dad Dog, Puppy)
- Story section: one scene from the first video (puppy by accident spot, Dad Dog comforting)
- Blog/reading section: three small illustrative plates for each post

Custom icon set: 6-8 icons in a warm terracotta line style (paw print, book, clock, heart, home, treat)

Logo/monogram: wordmark "The Pet Story Co." in Outfit with a small paw-print/heart mark

OG: branded 3:2 cover with brand name + tagline + hero character

## CTA inventory
1. **Primary CTA — "Read the story"** — pill-shaped, terracotta fill, cream text, slight hover lift
2. **Secondary CTA — "Browse recommendations"** — outline only, terracotta border and text
3. **Email CTA — "Send it to me"** — same pill shape as primary but in sage green
4. **Bridge page CTA — "Get the method"** — larger pill, terracotta, with small arrow indicator
